Built around NFPA 96

The code, in plain terms.

NFPA 96 sets the standard for ventilation control and fire protection of commercial cooking operations. Here is what three of its sections ask of your system, and how we handle each one.

NFPA 96 §8.1.6.3.1

Clean-out ports

Upblast fans need an access opening of at least 76 mm by 127 mm, or a 101 mm (4 in.) circular opening, so the fan blades can be cleaned and inspected.

We do: Clean-out port installation
NFPA 96 §8.1.1.1.1

Hinge kits

Upblast fans with motors in the airstream should be hinged, with flexible weatherproof electrical cable and service hold-open retainers, and be listed for that use.

We do: Hinge kit installation
NFPA 96 §11.6.1

Exhaust cleaning

When inspection finds grease-laden vapour deposits, the contaminated portion must be cleaned by a properly trained, qualified company acceptable to the authority having jurisdiction.

We do: Top to bottom exhaust cleaning
